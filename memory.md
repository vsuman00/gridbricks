## Adopted analytical policy

[ADR-002](docs/decisions/0002-timestamp-matched-reference.md) adopts `timestamp_matched_halfhour_reference_v1`. For each hourly WINDFOR target, select the greatest unambiguous publication at or before that decision's local cutoff. Compare it once with the latest unambiguous FUELHH `WIND` half-hour starting at exactly the target timestamp, inside the same snapshot. Keep the adjacent half-hour in storage but do not average, substitute, or duplicate it. The 60-minute forecast grid does not prove an hourly average; physical averaging equivalence remains unverified. Accuracy rows, UI, CSV and presentations state this limitation and carry `temporal_reference_mismatch`. `hourly_average_v1` is unsupported.

Store timestamps canonically in UTC; derive decision dates and cutoffs in Europe/London. A publication exactly at cutoff is eligible. Latest-publication conflicts stay unresolved with no older fallback. Missing is distinct from zero. Errors are forecast minus actual reference; pooled matched rows drive MAE, RMSE and bias, and no matches yield null metrics. Decision view never exposes later actuals or retrospective summary as knowledge at buying time. All responses and CSV pin one immutable snapshot and named policies.
